# Content policy

1. Every Approach has positive and negative applicability.
2. No fabricated scores, download counts, benchmark values, user reviews, prices or version numbers.
3. No near-duplicate Situation pages for Condition combinations.
4. Rule reasons are factual and conditional, not marketing copy.
5. Official resources explain current use; books explain concepts and must disclose outdated implementation scope.
6. AI output is a proposal until independent review, fact check, deterministic validation and pull-request review pass.
